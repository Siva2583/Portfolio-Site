About

Curiosity, with
engineering discipline.

I’m a Computer Science student who enjoys building software where the important decisions can be seen and checked.

That means treating money movement as ledger entries instead of mutable balances, recording algorithm states before playback, and verifying model-generated places rather than displaying coordinates on trust.

The projects span different domains, but I keep returning to one question: what should this system never assume?
